# Dashboard glossary

**Original criterion**: An acceptance requirement retained from the Owner's original scope, with a stable unique identity.

**Candidate**: The particular output being evaluated in an attempt. Acceptance of one candidate does not accept a later candidate.

**Attempt**: One preserved implementation and verification history for a candidate.

**Final acceptance**: A candidate-bound Planner decision supported by the required verification chain. Imported acceptance remains an assertion until its authoritative source is independently checked.

**Presentation projection**: A disposable, rebuildable view of original records. It does not replace the original evidence or CouncilFlow message contracts.

**Recorded active**: A role's last imported lifecycle state, without a claim that an independent process is currently running.

**Stale**: A snapshot whose last observation is missing or older than the stated freshness heuristic. Staleness is not evidence of a crash.
